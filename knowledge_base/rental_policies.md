# Rental Policies

## Security deposit
Most properties listed on RentWise require a security deposit equivalent to two to three months' rent, payable directly to the property owner before move-in. The exact amount is set by each individual owner and is not fixed by RentWise.

## Notice period
The standard notice period for vacating a rented property in Kerala is one month, unless the rental agreement between tenant and owner states otherwise. Always confirm the notice period in writing with the owner before signing.

## Maintenance responsibilities
For unfurnished and semi-furnished properties, tenants are typically responsible for minor day-to-day maintenance (light bulbs, minor plumbing), while owners handle structural repairs. For furnished properties, owners usually cover maintenance of provided furniture and appliances. Always clarify this with the specific owner, as practices vary.

## Pet policy
Only properties explicitly tagged with the "Pet Friendly" amenity allow pets. If a property does not list this amenity, assume pets are not permitted unless you confirm otherwise directly with the owner.

## Rent payment
RentWise does not process rent payments. Rent is paid directly between tenant and property owner, typically monthly, by whatever method both parties agree on (bank transfer is most common in Kerala).

## Visit etiquette
Arrive on time for scheduled visits. If you cannot make a scheduled visit, it's courteous to let the property contact know as early as possible, though RentWise does not currently support in-app cancellation.
