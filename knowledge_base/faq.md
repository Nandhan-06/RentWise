# RentWise FAQ

## How do I save a property?
Click the "Save" (heart) button on any property card. Saved properties appear on your Favorites page, where you can remove them at any time.

## How do I schedule a property visit?
Open a property card and click "Schedule visit." Pick a date, then click "Check availability" to see open time slots for that day. Visit slots run hourly from 10:00 to 17:00, with a break between 12:00 and 14:00. Once you pick a slot, your visit is confirmed immediately.

## Can I book two visits for the same property at the same time?
No. Once a time slot is booked by anyone for a property on a given date, that slot is no longer available to others. You'll see an error if you try to book a slot that was just taken.

## Can I cancel or reschedule a visit?
Version 1 of RentWise does not yet support cancelling or rescheduling a visit yourself. Contact the property's listed manager directly, or simply book a different available slot if you need a new time.

## How do I edit my profile?
Go to the Profile page from the navigation bar. You can update your name, phone number, and email address there. Changes save immediately.

## Is there a fee to use RentWise?
No. Browsing listings, saving favorites, scheduling visits, and using the AI search or chatbot are all free for renters.

## How accurate is the AI search?
The RentWise assistant only recommends properties that actually exist in RentWise's database — it turns your description into search filters and runs a real search, so it will never invent a listing that doesn't exist.
