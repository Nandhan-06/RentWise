# Amenities Guide

## Lift
An elevator serving the building's floors — relevant mainly for apartments above the second or third floor.

## Power Backup
The building has a generator or inverter system that keeps essential power (lights, fans, sometimes AC) running during outages, which are still common in parts of Kerala during monsoon season.

## Security
The property has a security guard, gated entry, or a monitored access system.

## Gym
An on-site fitness room, usually shared among residents of an apartment complex or gated community.

## Swimming Pool
A shared pool within the property complex, common in villas and larger gated apartment communities, less common in standalone apartments or PGs.

## Children Play Area
A designated outdoor or indoor play space for kids, typically found in larger gated communities.

## Clubhouse
A shared indoor community space for residents, often used for events, indoor games, or informal gatherings.

## Water Supply 24x7
Guarantees round-the-clock piped water supply, which is not universal in all areas of Kochi — worth checking for properties without this tag if reliable water access is a priority.

## Covered Parking
A dedicated, roofed parking spot, as opposed to open or street parking. Distinct from the general "parking" field on a listing, which just means a parking spot of some kind exists.

## Pet Friendly
The owner explicitly allows pets. Properties without this tag should be assumed pet-restricted unless confirmed otherwise with the owner.
