# Kochi Neighborhood Guide

## Kakkanad
Kakkanad is Kochi's main IT hub, home to Infopark and a large working professional population. It has a good mix of modern apartments and villas, decent traffic during office hours, and is a popular choice for tech employees who want a short commute.

## Edapally
Edapally is a well-connected junction area with excellent access to the metro, major hospitals, and shopping malls like Lulu Mall. It suits people who want convenience and don't mind a busier, more commercial neighborhood.

## Vytilla
Vytilla is a transport hub with strong bus and water metro connectivity, making it convenient for people commuting across the city. It's known for affordable shared and PG accommodation, popular with students and young professionals.

## Aluva
Aluva sits at the northern edge of Kochi, near the airport and the Aluva metro terminus. It offers a quieter, more residential feel with riverside properties, and tends to be more affordable than central Kochi.

## Marine Drive / MG Road
This is the commercial heart of the city, with sea-facing apartments, premium pricing, and walking access to shops, restaurants, and offices. Best suited for those who want to be in the middle of everything and don't mind paying more for it.

## Kumbalangi
Kumbalangi is a quieter, semi-rural area known for its backwaters and slower pace of life. It suits people who want more space and calm, at the cost of a longer commute into central Kochi.
